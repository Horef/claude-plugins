# Licences in this folder

The repository's MIT licence covers the code (`sciencefig/`, `benchmark/*.py`) and the figures the benchmark scripts generate. It does **not** cover the reused material, which keeps its own licence:

| Folder | Source | Licence | What it means here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/servier/` | Servier Medical Art (via Bioicons) | CC BY 3.0 | Free to use and adapt with credit; `assets.credits(c)` writes the caption line, "adapted" when recoloured |
| `assets/dbcls/` | DBCLS TogoTV (via Bioicons) | CC BY 4.0 | Same, with credit |
| `morphologies/swc/` | Blue Brain Project, NeuroMorpho.Org, OpenSourceBrain (per file in its README) | Mostly CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; NeuroMorpho files CC BY | **Non-commercial only**, credit the source and cite the original paper, share adaptations under the same licence. For a paper, prefer the NeuroMorpho.Org (CC BY) version of a cell |

Details and per-file sources: `assets/README.md`, `morphologies/swc/README.md`. Before adding material, check its licence and record it there.
