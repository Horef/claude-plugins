# Sources

The owner's copies (PDF and EPUB, in their DEVONthink library) were read in full or by chapter as noted in `writing-rules.md` and `story.md`. Nothing here reproduces them; the rules are paraphrased with chapter or rule numbers so a reader with the book can check them.

## Writing manuals (rules: `writing-rules.md`)
- Schimel J. 2012. *Writing Science: How to Write Papers That Get Cited and Proposals That Get Funded.* Oxford University Press. The story structures (OCAR, LD, LDR), gap framing, paragraph arcs, the 2-3-1 rule of emphasis. The core source.
- Mensh B, Kording K. 2017. Ten simple rules for structuring papers. *PLOS Computational Biology* 13(9): e1005619. Context-content-conclusion at every scale; results as declarative statements; Table 1 (diagnosis by reader symptom).
- Gopen GD, Swan JA. 1990. The science of scientific writing. *American Scientist* 78: 550-558. Topic and stress positions, old before new, subject-verb distance.
- Rougier NP, Droettboom M, Bourne PE. 2014. Ten simple rules for better figures. *PLOS Computational Biology* 10(9): e1003833.
- Nature. 2005 (annotated example). How to construct a Nature summary paragraph.
- Pinker S. 2014. *The Sense of Style.* Viking. Classic style; the curse of knowledge (ch. 3); syntax as a tree (ch. 4); arcs of coherence (ch. 5).
- Williams JM, Bizup J. 2017 (12th ed.). *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace.* Pearson. Characters and actions, cohesion and coherence, emphasis, motivation (condition plus cost), global coherence.

## Exemplars (techniques: `story.md`)
- Hofstadter DR. 1979. *Gödel, Escher, Bach: an Eternal Golden Braid.* Basic Books. The owner's model of a long argument told as a story.
- Hoffman DD, Prakash C. 2014. Objects of consciousness. *Frontiers in Psychology* 5: 577. A hard idea built up with definitions and signposts.
- The short, figure-led paper on thalamic activation of visual cortex at the single-synapse level (*Thalamic activation of the visual cortex at the single-synapse level*), as an example of main text carried by its figures.
- Goetz L, Roth A, Häusser M. 2021. Active dendrites enable strong but sparse inputs to determine orientation selectivity. *PNAS* 118: e2017339118. Figure-text integration in the field of dendritic computation.
