# Learnings: research-writing

Observations from use, written by the agents that used this skill; the strange-loop skill turns them into changes.
One line each: `- O<n> <date> [<kind>] <section>: <what happened, what would have helped> (<harness>, <context>)`.
Kinds: failure, friction, misled, missing, workaround, win, wish. ` ×N` = seen N times; ` → <version>` = resolved there.

## Open
- O1 2026-11-04 [win] 6 Readers: in the pilot (a 14-page modelling write-up) four fresh readers with only the PDF (3x Sonnet 5.5, 1x Gemini 4 Argon) all named the same weakest link (the title claimed more generality than the evidence supported); the revision changed the message and title, which no amount of polishing by the writer had done. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1)
- O3 2026-11-04 [friction] 6 Readers: round 1 brought 170 questions (49 must); one row per question was unworkable, grouping by theme (22 rows) worked; SKILL updated before release. Sorting took about an hour of deep-tier work; a light pre-pass that clusters questions by quoted page and keyword would cut it. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1)
- O6 2026-11-05 [win] 6 Readers: the outsider reader (Gemini Argon via agy, PDF only) caught a factual error carried over from the source write-up: the NMDA block was described as rate-matched but fired 4.4 vs 10 Hz at the design grating. Mixed model families paid off again. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1)
- O12 2026-11-05 [win] 6 Readers: round 2 on the combined write-up: the new editor role found number inconsistencies (text vs figure shares, contact counts, sweep vs calibrated gOSI) as well as prose; the Gemini outsider again caught a factual error (NMDA charge-matched range 0.5-0.8 should be 0.5-1.25) and that all follow-ups ran at 10x NMDA undisclosed. Mixed-family readers keep paying off. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1)

## Resolved
- O16 2026-11-09 [failure] 4 Figures: Figure 14 (fig_tools) and Figure 1 (fig_terms) were drawn in grey while the supplementary figures showing the same things (S1 cell variants, S2 encoder vs Durand) use colour; the owner found it strange (2026-11-09). Would help: a rule that a thing keeps its colour in main and supplement, and a check note for grey-only figures (simple_complex_v1) → 0.6.0
- O15 2026-11-09 [friction] 5 build: \figref inside a \caption is fatal (the figref macro records first citation), twice in this write-up; wr.py check could flag \figref in captions before the build (simple_complex_v1) → 0.6.0
- O14 2026-11-09 [misled] 4 Text: the write-up said 'the measured range', 'the measured V1 thalamic ratio' without saying who measured it or in what; the owner dislikes unattributed claims (2026-11-09). Would help: a rule to name every outside source where used, and a check note for 'measured/reported/known' without a citation (simple_complex_v1) → 0.5.0
- O13 2026-11-05 [missing] 4 Text/ledger: the ledger had no column for the condition a claim was tested under (here NMDA strength); the write-up silently mixed 2x headline and 10x follow-ups until a reader noticed. A 'condition' column per claim, and a check that each section states it, would catch this. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.3.0
- O11 2026-11-05 [friction] 6 Readers: in a project-foundations repo every file wr.py readers writes (briefs, later answers) fails docmap's Status-line check; twice now added by hand. wr.py could write a 'Status:' first line (or the skill could say to). (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.3.0
- O5 2026-11-05 [friction] 5 Build: preamble's placeins [section] left half-empty pages with 14 tall figures (27 pp); disabling it gave 23 pp but figures then land 2-3 pages after first citation. Would help: a figure-height budget in step 4 (e.g. at most ~0.55 textheight unless it is the answer figure) and a wr.py check that reports figure heights. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.3.0
- O2 2026-11-04 [wish] 5 Checks: a number mismatch (the text copied a rounded value from a results table; the figure printed the recomputed value, which rounds the other way) was caught only by looking at the page. A check that pulls the text of the figure PDFs (pdftotext) and lists numbers that appear in a figure and differently in the body would catch it; or a light-tier number tracer against the ledger's evidence. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.3.0
- O4 2026-11-04 [failure] 5 Build: wr.py build with a relative DIR (docs/writeups/x from the repo root) fails: latexmk writes into DIR/docs/writeups/x/build and the copy of build/main.pdf raises FileNotFoundError. Absolute DIR works. Resolve DIR to an absolute path first. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.2.0
- O7 2026-11-05 [failure] 6 Fresh readers: owner found prose problems no reader raised: telegraphic fragments ('Partly, in a narrow regime.'), skeleton instructions turned into text ('Before any method, the test that decides the question.'), vague references ('the published model', 'which have not been shown'). Reader briefs ask for content questions only; add an editor role that marks sentences that read as unnatural, compressed or ambiguous. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.2.0
- O8 2026-11-05 [missing] 3-4 Skeleton/text: no prose-voice layer. Rules (short, point first, plain words) applied literally by standard-tier writers give correct but 'inexpressive, inhuman' text; In short read as compressed notes, not an abstract. Needs: abstract shape (context, approach, result with numbers, meaning, self-contained terms), cohesion rules (old-before-new, transitions; Williams Style, Gopen & Swan 1990), structure devices (bold run-in paragraph heads, subsections), and a rule that technique names (T1 etc.) never surface as sentences. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.2.0
- O9 2026-11-05 [failure] 4 Figures: 'one encoding for one thing' over-applied: appendix figures with no wiring (cell, encoder) were drawn grayscale because hue was reserved for wirings. Owner wanted colour. Rule should say figures outside the encoding's domain get their own colours. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.2.0
- O10 2026-11-05 [missing] 7 Deliver/template: supplementary material should follow the references on a new page with its own title and S-numbered figures (owner's expectation, journal norm); template puts the appendix before the bibliography. (claude-code, simple_complex_v1) → 0.2.0
